# Dance Studio CRM

## Domain Description
The system models the operational activities of a dance studio, including the management of clients, teaching staff, schedules for group classes (categorized by dance style), studio rooms, and the status of bookings and attendance.

## System Scope
* **Students (Clients):** Storage of basic contact information and booking history.
* **Instructors:** Management of the coaching staff and their specializations by dance style.
* **Studios and Schedule:** Allocation of studio rooms to specific class time slots.
* **Bookings:** Recording student participation in specific classes and tracking booking status.
